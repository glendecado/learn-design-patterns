# Gang of Four (GoF) Design Patterns

## Why is it called "Gang of Four"?

The name **Gang of Four (GoF)** comes from the **four authors** of the famous book:

**Design Patterns: Elements of Reusable Object-Oriented Software**

The four authors are:

- Erich Gamma
- Richard Helm
- Ralph Johnson
- John Vlissides

They documented **23 classic object-oriented design patterns**, which became widely known as the **GoF Design Patterns**.

## Pattern Totals

| Type | Total |
|---|---:|
| Creational | 5 |
| Structural | 7 |
| Behavioral | 11 |
| **Total** | **23** |

### In short

**Gang of Four = 4 authors → 23 Design Patterns**
